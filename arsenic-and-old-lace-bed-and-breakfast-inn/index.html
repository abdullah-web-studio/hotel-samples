Welcome

A Select Registry Victorian Mansion

Arsenic and Old Lace Bed & Breakfast Inn features five unique luxury suites for your comfort and privacy, with unequaled service awaiting every guest. Choosing a Select Registry property ensures exceptional accommodations — and this inn is among the best in Eureka Springs and the Ozark Mountains. Relax on the wrap-around veranda overlooking beautiful gardens and the Arkansas hills, enjoy a multi-course gourmet breakfast each morning, and stroll the Historic Loop into downtown's shops, galleries and restaurants. The inn is also a beloved setting for small weddings and elopements.

The Mansion

A romantic Victorian mansion on Hillside Avenue — stained glass, gardens and veranda views over the Ozark hills.

Gourmet Breakfast

A delectable signature multi-course breakfast each morning, with fresh-baked cookies and cold drinks waiting in the dining room.

Weddings & Romance

Wedding and elopement packages in the garden, Victorian parlor or veranda — plus in-room experiences arranged on request.
