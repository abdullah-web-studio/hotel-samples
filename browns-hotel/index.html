Welcome to Browns Hotel

Just 200 yards from the waterfront, at the heart of picturesque Dartmouth, Browns is a small, stylish townhouse hotel, restaurant and bar. Sumptuous decor runs through the ground-floor reception, bar and restaurant, with nine smart, individually designed en-suite bedrooms above — the ideal base for the River Dart, the South Hams coastline, weddings and special celebrations.
