Welcome to the Seagull Inn

One of the original inns on the Northern California coast

Built in 1883 and welcoming travellers since the 1960s, the Seagull Inn sits in the heart of Mendocino Village — a short stroll from Mendocino Headlands and Big River State Parks, beaches, galleries, shops and fine dining.

Every guest room has its own private entrance and en suite bathroom, with views of the Pacific Ocean or our coastal gardens. Settle into the gardens, deck or patio, then wake to a generous organic breakfast brought straight to your room.
