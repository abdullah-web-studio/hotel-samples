Welcome to the Irish Cottage

Comfort meets character in Galena country

Your boutique escape awaits in Galena, where comfort meets character. Our curated rooms and suites blend contemporary comfort with distinctive Irish-inspired charm — rich wood panelling, warm hospitality and details you will not find in an ordinary hotel.

Here for a weekend getaway, a special celebration, a wedding, or a quiet moment away from the city? Unwind in the inn, gather in Frank O'Dowd's Pub & Grill, and stroll out to explore historic Galena.
