Welcome

Relax, Unwind & Explore Cornwall

Overlooking St Ives harbour, between golden beaches and a bustling scene of restaurants and art galleries, Trevose Harbour House is a small boutique guest house created by hosts Olivier & Angela — hoteliers by training and by passion.

Every room reflects a predominantly blue-and-white palette with bold retro styling balanced by bright, fresh influences — designed for serenity, calm and maximum comfort, with the finest luxuries and amenities.

Breakfast is a highlight: locally sourced, organic where possible, and served with care — the perfect start before the harbour, the beaches and the coastal path.
