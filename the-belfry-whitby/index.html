Welcome to The Belfry

A beautifully renovated Victorian home on Whitby's grand West Cliff, hosted by Phill & Sophie. Just a short stroll from the sandy beach, the town centre and the historic harbour.

Start the day with a freshly cooked Yorkshire breakfast made with local ingredients, then unwind in the guest lounge. Every guest receives a complimentary parking permit for their stay.

Our eight spacious en-suite rooms look out over St Hilda's Church or towards the sea — individually styled, with comfortable beds, flat-screen TVs and free Wi-Fi throughout.
